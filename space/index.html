What we take on

Vendor management and expediting

Open-order follow-up with machine shops, composite houses, electronics and component suppliers. Real ship dates, confirmed before pickup, and a heads-up the day one moves, with what it does to the build.

Parts and serial reconciliation

We match what was ordered, shipped and received across your buildings, vendors and storage, by part number, lot and serial, so a missing part shows up as a gap before integration, not during it.

Hardware moves

Air-ride and dedicated trucks, team drivers for time-critical runs, oversize permits and escorts, hazmat moves with qualified carriers, and drayage for imported parts through LA and Long Beach. Built around test stand and launch site dates.

Projects

Receiving and staging plans for a test campaign, a second-source search when a vendor can't keep up, and a move plan when a program changes sites.
