What we take on

Co-packers and suppliers

We confirm runs, ingredient and packaging arrivals, and pickups, and we tell you the day a date moves. We also open our network of co-packers, warehouses and packaging suppliers when you need a new one.

Inventory across every partner

We reconcile your co-packer, 3PL and distributor counts against what actually shipped, so your reorder is based on a real number.

Freight to the shelf

Drayage for imported ingredients and packaging, LTL and partials into KeHE, UNFI, Sprouts and Whole Foods DCs, full truckload, and reefer. Appointments watched, BOL and POD in your inbox the day it delivers.
