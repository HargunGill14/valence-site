About Valence

Built by operators. Run like it's our name on every load.

Valence is a boutique supply chain partner in Los Angeles. We take on suppliers, inventory and freight for companies that can't afford a surprise, and we do it as a small team that learns your business.

Why we started it

We spent years inside freight brokerages and supply chains watching the same thing happen. Companies got either a big firm that treated them like a ticket number or software that left them to do the work alone.

We built Valence to be the partner in between: hands on the work, every decision in yours.

The team
