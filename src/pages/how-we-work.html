How we work

We recommend. You approve. We execute.

Valence is a boutique supply chain partner that runs the work for you and keeps every decision in your hands. Three promises, on every page and in every load.

The three promises

  1. 01You see everything, all the time. Every PO, pallet and load, live, not in a Friday email.
  2. 02You know what it costs. Itemized quotes, accessorials up front, no line items you find later.
  3. 03You decide. We recommend, you approve, we execute. You set how much we handle on our own.

How visibility works

  • Live tracking on every truckload. Location and ETA, any hour.
  • LTL and drayage milestones. Pickup, terminal, out for delivery, delivered, and container status, as they happen.
  • Your portal. Every PO, load, count and document in one place, open any hour.
  • A person, always. Call or text any hour and a person answers. Our AI works inside your portal, sorting updates and flagging problems. It never stands between you and us.

How it shows up for you

  1. 01We gather it. POs, supplier emails, warehouse counts, carrier updates and portal data, wherever they live today.
  2. 02We connect it. Which PO feeds which load, which load feeds which shelf, which vendor feeds which build.
  3. 03We price it. Every problem arrives as dollars at risk, not a red dot.
  4. 04We act on it, with your OK. Each problem comes with a recommended fix. You approve it and we do it.
