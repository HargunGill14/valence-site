Cross-border bid lanes

Before

Labatt put its packaged beer lanes from US origins into its Canadian breweries out to a national over-the-road bid. Brewery receiving windows don't move, and every load crosses a border.

How it started

Our team put in for the US-to-Canada lanes when the bid opened.

What we saw

A truck outside a brewery's window waits, or goes back. On a cross-border lane the paperwork has to be right in both directions before the truck rolls.

What we changed

  • Won a share of the lanes.
  • Lined up paperwork in both directions before pickup.
  • Timed every truck to the brewery's receiving window.

Results

ResultUS-to-Canada beer lanes run for Labatt's breweries
