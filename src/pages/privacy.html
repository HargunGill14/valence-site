Privacy Policy

Effective October 26, 2026. What we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with.

1. Who we are

Valence Supply Chain LLC ("Valence," "we," "us") is a supply chain and freight logistics company based in California. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with, including our affiliate Substrate.

It covers our website at valencesupplychain.com, our customer portal, and our phone, email, and text communications with shippers, carriers, and truck drivers.

Substrate is a supply chain software company under common ownership with Valence. Substrate builds the software and data systems that power Valence's services, and it also provides supplier, inventory, and logistics services to customers.

2. Information we collect

We collect business and contact information needed to move freight and support our customers. We get it from you directly, from the carriers and shippers on a load, and from the tracking and service providers we work with.

Shippers and customers
Contact names, emails, phone numbers, company and billing details, shipment details like origins, destinations, commodities, and PO numbers. We also collect shipping documents like purchase orders, bills of lading, and invoices, emails and messages about your shipments, and any inventory or supplier information you share with us.
Suppliers and logistics partners
Contact and business details for the suppliers, vendors, warehouses, and customs brokers we work with for you, plus customs and cross-border shipping documents.
Customer portal users
Name, work email, role, and login details.
Carriers
Company name, MC and DOT numbers, insurance certificates, tax forms, payment details, and dispatcher contact information.
Truck drivers
Name, mobile number, truck and trailer numbers, and location during an active load when you accept a tracking link or share it on a check call. Carrier dispatchers usually give us a driver's number when they book a load with us.
Text messaging records
The date, time, and method of your consent to receive texts, and any opt-out requests.
Phone calls
We may record or transcribe calls for quality, training, and recordkeeping. When a call is recorded, we tell you at the start of the call.
Website visitors
Information you submit through our forms, plus basic technical data like IP address, browser type, and pages visited, collected through cookies and analytics tools.

3. How we use information

We use information to run shipments and the services around them. We do not sell personal information.

  • Arrange, dispatch, track, and deliver shipments
  • Send load updates, appointment changes, and tracking links by phone, email, or text
  • Vet carriers, verify insurance and operating authority, and prevent fraud
  • Invoice customers and pay carriers
  • Provide reporting, inventory reconciliation, and supplier management services
  • Operate, support, and improve our services and the Substrate platform
  • Meet legal, tax, insurance, and transportation recordkeeping requirements
  • Answer questions and support requests

4. How we share information

We share information with our affiliate Substrate and with the partners needed to move your freight. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We never share one customer's identifiable data with another customer.

Substrate
We share the information described in this policy with Substrate. Substrate uses it to run the software and data systems behind Valence, to deliver supplier, inventory, and logistics services to customers, and to develop and improve its products. Substrate handles this information under the same protections described in this policy.
Parties to a shipment
We share what is needed to complete a load. For example, shippers and receivers may see a driver's name, truck number, and tracking status, and carriers receive pickup and delivery details. On cross-border loads, we share shipment and customs documents with customs brokers and authorities.
Systems you connect
When you ask us to connect your ERP, warehouse, transportation, or other systems, we exchange data with those systems on your behalf.
Service providers
We use brokerage and back-office partners, tracking and visibility providers, payment and factoring providers, messaging providers, cloud hosting, and analytics tools. They may use information only to perform services for us.
De-identified data
We and Substrate may combine information into aggregated or de-identified form that no longer identifies you or your company. We may use and share that data for any purpose, such as improving our services and building industry benchmarks. Benchmarks and other insights shared across customers only use de-identified data. This policy does not limit our use of de-identified data.
Legal and safety
We may disclose information to comply with law or legal process, enforce our agreements, or protect the rights, property, or safety of Valence, our partners, or others.
Business transfers
If Valence is part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may transfer as part of that deal under this policy.

Mobile numbers and text messaging opt-in data follow the stricter rules in the next section, and those rules override anything in this list.

5. Text messaging (SMS) terms

By providing your mobile number and agreeing to receive texts, you consent to receive SMS messages from Valence Supply Chain. We text truck drivers, carriers, and customers only about shipments they are part of. That includes pickup and delivery details, appointment changes, check calls, and tracking links. We do not send marketing texts.

Opting in
We ask for your permission by phone, email, or in writing before we text you, and our first text confirms your opt-in.
Message frequency
Message frequency varies based on your shipments.
Costs
Message and data rates may apply.
Opting out
Reply STOP at any time. We will send one confirmation and then stop texting you.
Help
Reply HELP, or contact us at ops@valencesupplychain.com or (628) 212-9947.
Delivery
Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the sharing described in this policy excludes text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, and this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Our affiliate Substrate may access mobile numbers and opt-in records only to send and log shipment messages on behalf of Valence, never for marketing.

The full terms for our text messages are on our SMS Terms page.

6. Artificial intelligence

We use AI tools to help run and improve our services, such as reading shipment documents, tracking loads, and flagging problems early. Some of these tools come from third-party AI providers, which may process information only to provide services to us.

We do not allow third-party AI providers to use your information to train their general-purpose AI models. A person on our team reviews any AI-assisted decision that significantly affects you, and you can always reach a person when you call us.

7. Data retention and security

We keep information as long as we need it to provide our services, meet legal, tax, insurance, and transportation recordkeeping requirements, and resolve disputes. We keep records of text messaging consent and opt-outs for as long as needed to show we honored them. Deleted information may remain in backups for a limited time before it is overwritten.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, such as access controls and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a security breach affects your information, we will notify you as required by law.

8. Your choices and California privacy rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing ops@valencesupplychain.com. We may need to verify your identity first. We will respond within 30 days, though we may keep records the law requires us to keep. If you use our customer portal through your employer, we may send your request to your employer to handle.

Texts
Reply STOP to any message to opt out.
Emails
Use the unsubscribe link or reply asking us to stop.
Cookies
Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it. Google Analytics sets cookies and sends usage data, such as pages visited and general location, to Google; we have turned off its advertising features. We also use Microsoft Clarity to see how visitors use our pages through behavioral metrics, heatmaps and session replay. Clarity sets cookies and sends this usage data to Microsoft, which handles it under the Microsoft Privacy Statement at privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Our website does not currently respond to Do Not Track signals.
California residents
We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing.
Children
Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
