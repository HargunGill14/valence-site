Before

Anheuser-Busch hands out its freight through its own tendering system. Carriers compete for each load, and the receiving docks at breweries, wholesalers and craft breweries run on set windows.

How it started

Our team started taking AB's tendered loads in January 2021.

What we saw

Receiving docks plan their day around the truck. Wholesalers and craft breweries asked for the ETA on AB loads directly, because their own schedules hung on it.

What we changed

  • Took AB's loads lane by lane as they were tendered.
  • Ran them into AB's own breweries and out to the wholesalers that sell its beer.
  • Answered the receiving docks directly when they asked where a load was.
