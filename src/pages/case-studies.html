Canadian Canning · Team experience before Valence

Cans into co-packers on the day the run needed them.

Client
Canadian Canning
Service
Inbound coordination
Market
Canada and US

The problem

Empty cans into co-packers, where a late truck idles the line and an early one has nowhere to unload.

ChallengeCan arrivals tied to the co-packer run date
ChallengeLoads across the border and across the country
ChallengeCo-packers with no yard space

Where their team was

TraitPackaging supplier shipping to many co-packers
TraitEach co-packer with its own schedule
TraitFreight booked load by load

What we did

We tied each load to the run it fed, booked delivery to the day the line needed it and confirmed receipt with the co-packer.

ActionEvery load tied to a run date
ActionDelivery timed to the line, not the truck
ActionReceipt confirmed with the co-packer

Outcomes

ProvenOn the day
cans arrived for the run they fed
ProvenNo line held for a truck
ProvenFewer calls between supplier and co-packer
