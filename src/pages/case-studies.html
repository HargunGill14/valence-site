A private-label soda brand · Team experience before Valence

One founder, 945 loads, two retailers that don't forgive a missed window.

Client
A private-label soda brand, selling into Trader Joe's and Aldi
Service
Freight, retailer compliance, co-packer inbound
Market

Size
Founder-run
Duration
April 2023 to December 2025

The problem

Before working with us, one founder ran the whole supply chain by email: 945 loads, 49 lanes, 308 carriers and two co-packer flows into Trader Joe's and Aldi.

ChallengeThe co-packer ran night shifts on inbound cans nobody could see coming.
ChallengeTrader Joe's DC desks need the PO, BOL and bioterrorism sheet, with 24 hours' notice for changes.
ChallengeThe weekly plan lived in a shared sheet that changed after cutoff, sometimes the same morning.

What we did

Our team ran the brand's national freight end to end: inbound cans to the plant, finished goods into the retailers' DCs, and the paperwork each desk asked for.

ActionKept the co-packer fed with more than 430 inbound loads from its can supplier, on a schedule that ran night shifts.
ActionManaged PO-specific appointments, BOLs and bioterrorism paperwork on every Trader Joe's load.
ActionCovered the freight with 308 vetted carriers across 44 pickup and delivery points, so no lane depended on one truck.

Outcomes

loads and $1.29M in freight across 49 lanes in under three years
Proven149

What the record showed

We went back through our own record of the account afterward. The gaps a good broker still leaves are the ones Valence is built around.

Found20.6 emails and 3 threads per load, 18,105 emails behind 945 loads. 27% of threads were one question: where's my truck.
FoundProof of delivery on file for 23% of loads. $27,500 in detention and re-rates paid above the confirmed rate, none approved ahead of time.
Found$13,398 in delivered loads never billed or paid short, found in a single audit.
