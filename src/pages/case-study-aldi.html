Before

Aldi carries far fewer products than a typical grocer, so an empty slot shows. Its DCs run tight appointment windows, and a twice-yearly over-the-road bid decides which carriers get the freight.

How it started

Our team started moving freight into Aldi in 2021.

What we saw

Aldi buys freight on a schedule. The bid sets the lanes and the backup carriers, and spot loads go to carriers already in the mix.

What we changed

  • Earned a place as a backup carrier on Aldi's over-the-road bid.
  • Handled contract and spot loads into Aldi DCs.
  • Booked every delivery to the DC's appointment.

Results
