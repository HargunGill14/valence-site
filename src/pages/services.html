01

Supplier and partner management

Everything between "we need it" and "it's on our dock," for co-packers, manufacturers, machine shops, packaging and ingredient suppliers, warehouses and equipment vendors.

  • Onboarding and vetting. Insurance certificates, W-9s, quality certifications (SQF, BRC, AS9100 and the like), facility and food safety registrations, and reference checks before the first PO.
  • PO management. Issue POs, chase acknowledgments, confirm quantities, prices and ship dates, and handle change orders and cancellations.
  • Production and capacity booking. Book co-packer runs and vendor capacity ahead of demand, and track MOQs and lead times by item.
  • Expediting. Weekly or daily follow-up on open orders, ship-date confirmation, advance ship notices, and escalation the day a date moves.
  • Inbound coordination. Packaging, ingredients and components lined up to land before the run or the build, not after.
  • Quality and claims. Nonconformances, rejected lots, shortages and damage worked through to a credit, a replacement or a rework.
  • Documents and traceability. Certificates of analysis, spec sheets, lot codes, serial numbers and the paper trail auditors and retailers ask for.
  • Scorecards. On-time, in-full, quality and responsiveness by supplier, reviewed with you every month.
  • Sourcing and backups. RFQs, second sources, and introductions from our network of co-packers, manufacturers and warehouses when one partner can't keep up.
  • One point of contact. Your suppliers talk to us, we talk to you.

02

Inventory reconciliation

One number you can trust, across every place your inventory sits or moves.

  • Count matching. Co-packer, 3PL, distributor DCs, Amazon, your own warehouse and job sites, reconciled against each other on a set schedule.
  • Three-way match. PO against what shipped (BOL, ASN) against what was received (receipt, POD), line by line.
  • In-transit inventory. What's on the water, on the rail and on the road, counted where it really is.
  • Lot, date code and serial tracking. First-expired-first-out for food, serialized and traceable parts for aerospace and equipment.
  • Short-dated and aging stock. Flagged early enough to sell, move or donate it.
  • Variance investigation. When counts don't match, we find out why, receiving errors, shrink, damage, mis-picks, and get it corrected at the source.
  • Cycle counts. Scheduled with your partners, with the results checked.
  • Days of supply and reorder points. By SKU or part, by location, so reorders are based on real numbers.
  • Rebalancing. Transfers between DCs or sites when one runs long and another runs short.
  • Holds and quarantines. Quality holds and damaged stock tracked until they're released or written off.
  • Month-end close. Book versus physical tied out for your accountant, so COGS is right.
  • 3PL invoice audit. Storage, handling and accessorial charges checked against your rate card.

03

Freight

Every mode, booked, tracked and documented by the same team.

  • Drayage. Port and rail drayage nationwide, with container tracking and last-free-day, demurrage and per diem watched so fees don't pile up.
  • Truckload. Dry van, reefer, flatbed, step deck, RGN and conestoga.
  • LTL, volume LTL and partials. For brands shipping a few pallets at a time, with consolidation when it saves money.
  • Specialized. Oversize with permits and escorts, hazmat with qualified carriers, air-ride, team drivers, dedicated and expedited.
  • Cross-border. US and Canada, both directions.
  • Retailer compliance. Appointments, must-arrive-by dates and on-time, in-full rules for big-box and grocery DCs.
  • Paperwork. BOL, POD, photos and temperature logs sent the day it delivers.
  • Claims. Overage, shortage and damage claims filed and followed through.
