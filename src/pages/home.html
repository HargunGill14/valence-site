How it works

Not a normal brokerage. The software is why it's fast.

A broker opens your email, calls three carriers and gets back to you tomorrow. Here, your two ZIPs land in a system that already has our lane history in front of it: the carriers we have run on that corridor and what they cost last time. That is why your quote comes back fast, not tomorrow.

  1. 01

    Quoted fast, because of the system.

    Your request arrives with the lane, the equipment and our own history on that corridor already pulled up: carriers we have run it with, what they charged, which ones delivered on time. Your quote comes back by email, with accessorials itemized.

  2. 02

    Booked and watched, by the software and the team.

    Appointment set, carrier confirmed, check-calls logged as they come in. The system flags a truck that goes quiet or a window a receiver moved; a person makes the call. You hear about it once it is fixed, in plain language, not a status code.

    You never deal with the carrier, the driver or the dispatcher. One number, any hour.

  3. 03

    Proof lands with the load.

    The signed POD and BOL are in your account the day it delivers. Weeks later, when a retailer short-pays, the proof is already matched to the PO, and the dispute is drafted for your approval. Nothing goes out without your yes.

    Deductions and chargebacks recovery is coming soon; the documents are live today.
