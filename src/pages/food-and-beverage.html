Questions founders ask

What is OTIF?

On time, in full. It is the share of what you ship that arrives inside the retailer's window and complete. Walmart scores it case by case, so one short pallet can put a whole order out of compliance.

What does Walmart charge for a late or short delivery?

3% of the cost of goods on every case that misses. Walmart expects 90% of cases on time when you book the freight, 98% ready at pickup when Walmart books it, and 95% in full. The fine comes off automatically, so most brands only see it on the remittance.

What happens if my truck misses its Costco appointment?

Depots may wait about 30 minutes, but they don't have to. A late truck is often turned away, the next appointment is usually 24 to 48 hours out, and chargebacks for the missed appointment come back to you, whoever the carrier was.

Do Sprouts, Aldi and Trader Joe's fine for late deliveries?

They don't publish a fine schedule. Their rules sit in your vendor agreement and routing guide, and the cost of missing them is rejected loads and a buyer who starts to doubt you. On a small shelf, an empty slot shows.

How does Valence keep us inside the window?

We confirm your product is ready before we book the truck, book and protect the appointment, check the retailer's rules before pickup, and track every load live. When a fine lands anyway, the BOL and POD are already filed so you can fight it.
