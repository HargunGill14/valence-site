SMS Terms

Effective October 14, 2026. These terms cover text messages from Valence Supply Chain LLC ("Valence Supply Chain").

1. Messages we send

By providing your mobile number and agreeing to receive texts, you consent to receive SMS messages from Valence Supply Chain. We text truck drivers, carriers, and customers only about shipments they are part of, such as pickup and delivery details, appointment changes, check calls, tracking updates, and requests for shipping documents. We do not send marketing texts.

2. How you opt in

We ask for your permission by phone, email, or in writing before we text you. Our first text confirms your opt-in.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your shipments.

4. Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details about your plan.

5. Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. We will send one confirmation and then stop texting you. To start again, reply START.

6. Help

Reply HELP to any message, email ops@valencesupplychain.com, or call (628) 212-9947.

7. Carrier liability

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

8. Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Read our full Privacy Policy.
